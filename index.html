We strongly recommend starting 8 to 12 months before your desired intake cycle. The majority of universities feature two major intakes: Autumn/Fall (September/October) and Spring (January/February). Starting early guarantees you secure competitive merit scholarships, complete English proficiency exams (IELTS/PTE), and comfortably clear visa processing times.
